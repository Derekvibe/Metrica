A NUMBER ISN'T A VERDICT

BMI is a starting point,
not the whole story.

BMI compares weight with height. It doesn't directly measure body fat, muscle, or overall health, and it may not be a useful measure for everyone. Use it as one piece of context — never as a diagnosis.
