01

Information involved

The mobile app lets you enter height and weight, calculates a BMI estimate, and can save check-ins with their date. It also remembers your metric or US unit preference. In the current source implementation, these app records are stored locally on your device; there is no account, cloud-sync, or app server feature that receives them.

The website calculator performs its calculation in your browser. The website code provided with this project does not submit or save the numbers you enter.

02

How information is used

Measurements are used to calculate and display BMI and, if you choose, to show your check-in history and trend. The website calculator uses the entered values only to update the result on the current page.

03

Sharing & third-party services

The current app source has no measurement upload, account, or synchronization feature. However, the publisher has not yet verified whether the final mobile build, platform services, analytics, advertising, or other third-party SDKs collect information. That review and any required disclosures are outstanding.

A website hosting provider may process basic technical request information such as IP address and access time in its server logs. The selected host and its retention practices have not been confirmed for this draft.

04

Storage & retention

App check-ins remain in local app storage until you clear them in Settings or remove the app. Device backups and operating-system behavior may affect how long local data is retained. The website calculator does not retain entered values through the provided page code.

05

Your choices

You can clear the app’s saved check-in history from Settings. You can also choose not to enter measurements in either calculator. Add a working privacy contact below so people can ask questions or make requests about their information.

06

Health information & intended use

Height, weight, and BMI can be sensitive personal information. BMI is a general screening measure and is not medical advice, a diagnosis, or a substitute for speaking with a qualified health professional. Consider who can access your device before saving measurements.
